OUR ROOTS

Our Story

Prayas Coaching Centre was established in 2022 in Pulpara, near Janakalyan L.P. School, Village Kabaitari Pt-IV, Bongaigaon District, Assam.

Foundational Belief

"Every student possesses hidden potential."

Many students in rural communities never receive the quality guidance they deserve. Prayas was created to bridge that gap by providing affordable, quality education and helping students build confidence, strong fundamentals and academic excellence.

Focusing on Every Learner

Instead of focusing only on already high-performing students, Prayas believes in helping average and struggling students become confident achievers.

  • Rooted in Bongaigaon District, Assam
  • Affordable and accessible learning environment
  • Continuous mentoring from Class VIII to X
